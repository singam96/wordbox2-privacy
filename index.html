Wordbox 2 – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Wordbox 2 is a word puzzle game by Sachin George. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or microphone, and it does not request internet access.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (solved puzzles, stars, unlocked topics) and your settings (sound, vibration, reduced motion) are saved in a small file on your own device. It never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or use "Reset progress" in the app's settings.

Permissions

The app asks only for the vibration permission, so tiles can give a small buzz when you play. You can turn this off in the app's settings.

Third parties

The app uses no third-party services, SDKs or advertising networks, and shares no data with anyone.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children either. All words and content are chosen to be family-friendly.

Changes
